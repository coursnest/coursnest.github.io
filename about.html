OUR PURPOSE

Why CoursNest exists

There is no shortage of information online. The harder part is finding a clear path through it: a starting point, a few useful ideas and enough structure to keep going. CoursNest brings those pieces together in short, practical courses for people learning independently.

How learning works here

Choose from 30 courses across marketing, money, business, technology and personal development. Each course is divided into modules and lessons, with examples, takeaways and an optional quiz. You can mark lessons complete as you go.

Your progress is stored in your browser using local storage. That means there is no account to create and no personal profile for us to manage. If you clear browser data or change devices, local progress may not follow you.

Built for real life

CoursNest is not a university, a certification authority or a replacement for professional advice. It is an open educational resource: a place to learn the basics, ask better questions and decide what you want to explore next.

Everything is free to browse, read and use. If a lesson helps, pass it on to someone else who might find it useful.
