How does email marketing work?

Email marketing is a permission-based relationship: someone chooses to hear from you, and you keep making that choice worthwhile.

Set expectations

Explain what people will receive and how often. Clear expectations make a welcome message feel like an agreement rather than a surprise.

Send something useful

A useful email can teach, point, compare or help someone take a next step. Not every message needs to ask for a purchase.

Measure with respect

Look at engagement as a signal, not a verdict. Replies, clicks, unsubscribes and questions can all teach you what to improve.
