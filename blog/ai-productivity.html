How AI can improve productivity

AI is most useful when it reduces friction around work you already understand, while you keep responsibility for the decision.

Use it for a first pass

Ask for an outline, a list of alternatives or a clearer version of your own notes. The value is often in moving from a blank page to something you can inspect.

Give it context and constraints

State the goal, audience, format and limits. A clear task usually produces a more useful starting point than a vague request for “something good.”

Keep review in the loop

Check claims, privacy and tone before sharing. Fluency is not proof, and speed is not the same as quality.
