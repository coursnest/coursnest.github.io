What is SEO, really?

SEO is the practice of making useful pages easier for both people and search engines to understand and discover.

The simple model

Search systems discover pages, store information about them and try to match results with what a person is trying to accomplish. You cannot control every result, but you can make your page clear, useful and trustworthy.

Start with intent

A search phrase is not the whole question. Someone searching for “budgeting basics” may want a template, an explanation or a first lesson. A good page makes its purpose obvious and answers one intent well.

Clarity beats tricks

Use descriptive titles, logical headings, useful internal links and original explanations. Avoid repeating a keyword until the page becomes harder to read.
