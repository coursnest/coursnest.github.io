What makes content marketing useful?

Useful content helps a specific person make progress on a question they already care about.

Start with the question

Instead of asking what you can publish, ask what your reader is trying to understand, compare or do. A clear question gives the piece a useful boundary.

Make a promise

Tell the reader what they will leave with. A small, honest promise is easier to deliver than a giant claim.

Build a repeatable rhythm

Choose a pace you can maintain and review what people use, save or ask next. Content becomes a system through repetition and learning.
