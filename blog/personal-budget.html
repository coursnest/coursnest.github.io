A personal budget you can actually keep

A useful budget is a plan that helps you choose. It is not a test of discipline or a demand for a perfect month.

Start with visibility

List income and recurring costs first. Then add flexible categories and expenses that arrive less often. A number you can see is easier to plan for than a surprise you avoid.

Give the plan room

Life has variable weeks. Leave room for choices and add a small buffer where possible. A budget that leaves no space often creates a rebound instead of a habit.

Review with curiosity

Once a week or month, compare the plan with reality. Ask what changed, what you learned and what one small adjustment would make the next period easier.
