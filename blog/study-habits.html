How to build better study habits

The most useful study habit is not “study more.” It is making each session answer a clear question.

Replace review with retrieval

Close the notes and try to explain the idea, solve a problem or answer a question. The effort shows you what is available without looking.

Return over time

Spacing your practice gives you more than one chance to remember. Short returns across several days are often more useful than one long night.

Reflect once, then adjust

After a session, write what helped, where you got stuck and what you will try next. Keep the reflection small enough that it becomes part of the habit.
