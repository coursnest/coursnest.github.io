What is financial literacy?

Financial literacy is the ability to understand everyday money information well enough to ask useful questions and make informed choices.

It is more than vocabulary

Knowing terms such as interest, fees, inflation and risk helps, but literacy also means comparing options, noticing assumptions and understanding how a choice fits your goals.

Use questions as tools

Ask: What is the total cost? What can change? What happens if circumstances change? Which part is a fact, and which part is a prediction?

Pause when the pressure rises

Urgency can make ordinary choices feel like now-or-never decisions. A pause gives you room to check evidence, alternatives and your own purpose.
