Privacy, stated plainly.

This policy describes the current static CoursNest website.

Last updated: September 2026

What the site stores

CoursNest uses your browser’s local storage to remember course progress, quiz scores and your language preference. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to a CoursNest server.

What the site does not collect

The basic site does not require an account, password, payment details, newsletter subscription or analytics account. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses or precise location through the learning experience.

Contact

If you use the email link on the Contact page, your email provider will handle that message. The site itself does not provide a backend inbox.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising or other services are added later, this policy should be updated before those services are enabled.
